# Mheni Studios working principles

Mheni Studios is built by a solo founder alongside full-time employment. Keep changes focused, sustainable, and easy to operate.

- Prefer the smallest effective implementation.
- Avoid unnecessary infrastructure and operational complexity.
- Put product quality, user trust, retention, and sustainability before short-term monetisation.
- Do not introduce dependencies, services, paid tooling, or infrastructure without a clear reason.
- Avoid large architectural rewrites unless they are necessary.
- Preserve working functionality. Work incrementally and verify changes.
- Keep each product individually branded, with Mheni Studios as the umbrella studio.
- Do not assume every product needs its own social presence, backend, or infrastructure stack.
